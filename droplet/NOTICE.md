## Map

The widget's dot map is sampled from [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com)'s 1:110m land, through [world-atlas](https://github.com/topojson/world-atlas) (ISC). Natural Earth is in the public domain. The grid is stored in `Sources/DotquadLite/Core/WorldDots.swift`; nothing is fetched.

## Data

The answers come from dotquad.app, which builds them from IP data published by others:

- IP address data powered by [IPinfo](https://ipinfo.io) (CC BY-SA 4.0).
- [IP Geolocation by DB-IP](https://db-ip.com) (CC BY 4.0).
